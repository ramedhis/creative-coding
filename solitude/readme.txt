solitude
--------

Three (var2: four) concentric spheres of points that ripple, rotate, blink, and pulse at 
different speeds, rendered in 3D on a black background.

- `var1`: original version, 700×700, 500 vertices per sphere.
- `var2`: refactored version, 1000×1000, 800 vertices per sphere, adds a pulsing center sphere, 
  and loops cleanly over a fixed frame count.
- `var2_for_output`: same as var2, with frame-by-frame PNG export added for rendering out a video/GIF.
